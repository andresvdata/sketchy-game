# Code of Conduct

Sketchy is a game about making friends laugh. We want the community around it to feel the same: welcoming, kind and fun.

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Keep prompts and content family friendly. Funny, never cruel.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Prompts or content that target real people or groups
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a friendly, professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (a direct message, or the repository's private security reporting if it's enabled). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
